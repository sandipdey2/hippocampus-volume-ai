Subject: Introduction to HippoVolume.AI – Automated Hippocampal Volume Measurement

Dear Colleague,

I am writing to introduce HippoVolume.AI, an automated tool that measures hippocampal volume from routine T2-weighted MRI scans. Because hippocampal atrophy is a sensitive marker of Alzheimer’s disease progression, reliable volume numbers can help you track patients over time and support clinical decision-making.

What the algorithm does
The software locates the hippocampus on the MRI, segments the anterior and posterior portions, and calculates three volumes in cubic millimeters:
- Anterior hippocampus
- Posterior hippocampus
- Total hippocampus

These numbers appear in a simple report that is automatically sent back to the PACS and can be viewed in OHIF (or any DICOM viewer) alongside the original images.

Performance
On an independent test set the algorithm achieved a mean Dice coefficient of 0.90 and a mean Jaccard index of 0.82 compared with expert segmentations. In practical terms, the volume measurements are typically within about 10 % of manual measurements for standard-quality T2 scans.

How to use it
Once a study is received by the PACS, the AI module runs automatically (or on demand). You will see a new series labeled “HippoVolume.AI Report” containing the volume numbers and a few reference slices. No extra clicks are required beyond opening the study.

Limitations and appropriate use
The tool is designed for diagnostic-quality axial T2 MRI with slice thickness ≤ 2 mm that adequately covers the medial temporal lobe. It may be less reliable on scans with severe motion, large artifacts, or unusual anatomy (e.g., post-surgical changes). The report is intended as a quantitative aid; final interpretation remains with the radiologist.
